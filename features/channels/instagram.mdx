# Instagram

ChatterMate is an **official Meta integration** for Instagram. Connect a **professional Instagram account** and your AI agent answers the direct messages people send it, in the same **Conversations** inbox as every other channel, with human handover.

There are two ways to connect:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sign in with Instagram" icon="instagram">
    ChatterMate Cloud. Instagram Business Login connects your account — no Meta app, no tokens, and no Facebook Page needed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enter credentials manually" icon="key">
    Self-hosted, or anyone using their own Meta app. Paste an Instagram account ID and the linked Page's access token.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
**On ChatterMate Cloud, connecting is a few clicks.** Instagram Business Login connects
your account under ChatterMate's reviewed app, so there is **no Meta app to create, no
access token to generate, and no webhook to configure** — sign in with Instagram,
assign an agent, and you're live. It's open to every Cloud account. The manual path
below is for self-hosters and anyone who prefers their own Meta app.
</Info>

## Prerequisites

- An Instagram **Business** or **Creator** account (not personal).
- **Only for the manual path:** the account must also be **linked to a Facebook Page** (Instagram → Settings → linked accounts, or from the Page's settings), because the manual path authenticates with that Page's token.

<Note>
Signing in with Instagram needs **no Facebook Page at all**. It's Instagram Business Login — a different sign-in from the Facebook one WhatsApp and Messenger use — so the business signs in with Instagram itself and the resulting token belongs to the Instagram account.
</Note>

## Connect by signing in with Instagram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Instagram card">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **Connect** on the **Instagram** card, then **Continue with Instagram**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/instagram-connect-instagram.webp" alt="The Connect Instagram dialog offering Continue with Instagram" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and grant access">
    Instagram's sign-in opens in a popup. Sign in as the professional account you want ChatterMate to answer for, and approve access to read the account and manage its messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign an AI agent">
    Instagram Login grants exactly one account, so there's nothing to pick — choose the agent that answers these DMs and click **Assign agent**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
ChatterMate subscribes the account to the Instagram messaging webhooks for you, so there is nothing to configure in the Meta dashboard. The connection shows up as your `@username`.
</Note>

<Tip>
Instagram access tokens expire eventually, unlike a System User token. If DMs stop reaching the inbox after a long stretch, reconnect from the Instagram card — signing in again refreshes the credentials in place.
</Tip>

## Connect with your own Meta app

Use this if you self-host, or would rather keep the integration under your own Meta app. This path uses the Facebook Graph API and the linked Page's token, so the Instagram account **must** be linked to a Facebook Page.

### Step 1 — Set up a Meta app

If you already created a Meta app for WhatsApp or Messenger, skip to step 2.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Business app">
    Go to [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com) → **My Apps → Create App** → **Business**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business Verification">
    Complete Business Verification in [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com) → **Security Center**. Start it first — it gates production access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Instagram + Messenger">
    Add the **Instagram** product and link your professional account (Instagram messaging is configured through the Messenger/Instagram settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the webhook">
    Set the callback URL to `https://<your-domain>/api/v1/webhooks/meta` with your `META_WEBHOOK_VERIFY_TOKEN`, and subscribe to Instagram **messages**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2 — Copy your credentials

| Credential | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| **Instagram account ID** | The IG account's numeric ID (e.g. `17841400000000000`), from the Graph API or the linked-account settings. |
| **Linked page access token** | The access token for the Facebook Page the Instagram account is linked to (System User token for a permanent one). |

### Step 3 — App Review (to message real customers)

Submit **App Review** for:

- `instagram_basic`
- `instagram_manage_messages`
- (plus `pages_show_list`, `business_management`)

Include a narrated **screencast** showing a customer DMing your Instagram account and ChatterMate replying.

### Step 4 — Connect in ChatterMate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Instagram card">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **Connect** on the **Instagram** card, then **Enter credentials manually instead**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Paste the **Instagram account ID** and the **Linked page access token**. ChatterMate encrypts them and verifies inbound webhook signatures.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/instagram-manual-credentials.webp" alt="Entering an Instagram account ID and linked page access token in ChatterMate" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign an AI agent">
    Choose the AI agent that answers Instagram and click **Assign agent**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If the account isn't a professional account linked to a Page, connecting fails — ChatterMate surfaces the reason so you can fix the link and retry.
</Note>

## Test it

DM your Instagram account. If you signed in with Instagram on ChatterMate Cloud, DM it from any account — app review is already done. If you connected with **your own** Meta app, only accounts with a role on that app can reach it until your own app review completes.

The AI agent replies, and the conversation appears in **Conversations** with an **Instagram** badge and human handover.
