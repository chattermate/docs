# CRM Sync

CRM Sync sends the leads your agents capture straight into your CRM. When a visitor is captured (see [Lead Capture](/features/lead-capture)), ChatterMate creates or updates the matching contact in **HubSpot** or **Pipedrive**, deduplicated by email, and attaches the AI qualification summary as a note.

It's a **one-way push** (ChatterMate → your CRM). Nothing is read back or overwritten in ChatterMate.

<Note>
On ChatterMate Cloud, CRM Sync is a **Pro** feature. Self-hosted deployments have it built in — you just register your own OAuth apps (see [Self-hosted setup](#self-hosted-setup)).
</Note>

## What gets pushed

| ChatterMate | HubSpot | Pipedrive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email (dedupe key) | Contact, upserted by email | Person, matched by email |
| Name, phone, company | Contact properties | Person + linked Organization |
| Lifecycle | `lifecyclestage: lead` | An open **Lead** on the person |
| AI summary, custom fields, source page | Note on the contact | Note on the lead |

Repeat captures of the same email **update** the existing record instead of creating duplicates. Pipedrive won't open a second lead if the person already has one open, so a returning visitor never spams your leads inbox.

## Connecting a CRM (Cloud)

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and find the **CRM** section:

1. Click **Connect** on HubSpot or Pipedrive.
2. Authorize ChatterMate in the CRM's OAuth screen.
3. You're returned to the Integrations page and the card shows **Connected** with your account name.

<Note>
One CRM account connects to one ChatterMate organization. Connecting requires the **Manage Organization** permission.
</Note>

## Turning on sync for an agent

Connecting a CRM doesn't move any leads on its own — you choose per agent which CRM its captured leads go to.

Open an agent → **Lead Capture** tab → **Sync to CRM**, and pick **None**, **HubSpot**, or **Pipedrive**.

- Every lead that agent captures is then queued for the selected CRM automatically.
- If you pick a CRM that isn't connected yet, ChatterMate saves the choice and shows an inline warning with a link to Settings → Integrations — connect it whenever, and syncing starts from the next capture.

<Tip>
The agent's selection is respected on **automatic** sync — a lead only goes to the CRM you picked for that agent, not to every connected CRM.
</Tip>

## Manual "Sync now" for a person

You can also push an individual person on demand. Open **People**, select a person, and in the detail drawer use **Sync to CRM**. This pushes them to **every** connected CRM and shows where they've already been synced, with a link to the created record.

Use it to backfill people captured before a CRM was connected, or to re-push after fixing details.

## Failure handling

CRM problems never interrupt a conversation. Syncs run in the background and retry with backoff:

- **Not connected** — no lead is queued; the agent's Lead Capture tab shows an inline warning.
- **Temporary errors** (rate limits, network) — retried automatically; nothing for you to do.
- **Expired connection** — the integration card shows **Reconnect**. Reconnecting automatically re-queues the leads that failed while it was down.
- **Permanent failures** — surfaced as a recent-failure count on the integration card.

## Self-hosted setup

Self-hosted ChatterMate has CRM Sync built in with **no plan restriction** — you bring your own OAuth apps so tokens stay in your infrastructure.

### 1. Register an OAuth app

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="HubSpot">
    Create an app in the [HubSpot developer portal](https://developers.hubspot.com):

    - **Redirect URL:** `https://<your-backend-domain>/api/v1/crm/hubspot/callback`
    - **Scopes:** `crm.objects.contacts.read crm.objects.contacts.write`

    Copy the app's **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pipedrive">
    Create an app in the [Pipedrive Developer Hub](https://developers.pipedrive.com):

    - **Callback URL:** `https://<your-backend-domain>/api/v1/crm/pipedrive/callback`
    - **Scopes:** `contacts:full`, `leads:full`, `search:read`

    Copy the app's **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The redirect/callback URL is derived from your `BACKEND_URL`, so make sure that's set to your public backend origin.

### 2. Set the credentials

Add the relevant variables to `backend/.env` (only the CRMs you want to enable):

```bash
# HubSpot
HUBSPOT_CLIENT_ID=your-hubspot-client-id
HUBSPOT_CLIENT_SECRET=your-hubspot-client-secret

# Pipedrive
PIPEDRIVE_CLIENT_ID=your-pipedrive-client-id
PIPEDRIVE_CLIENT_SECRET=your-pipedrive-client-secret
```

### 3. Run the sync worker

CRM pushes are processed by a dedicated worker. Add it to your Compose stack alongside the backend:

```yaml
crm_sync:
  image: chattermate/backend:latest
  command: python -m app.workers.crm_sync
  env_file:
    - ./backend/.env
  depends_on:
    db:
      condition: service_healthy
  restart: always
```

<Note>
The worker polls a database queue — no Celery or extra broker needed. For Pipedrive it also runs a periodic token-refresh sweep so idle connections don't lapse (Pipedrive refresh tokens expire after 60 idle days).
</Note>

Once the credentials are set and the worker is running, connect and configure exactly as in the Cloud sections above — the **CRM** cards appear on Settings → Integrations automatically.
