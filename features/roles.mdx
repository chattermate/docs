## How Permissions Are Enforced

Every permission is checked in two places, and both have to agree:

- **The interface** hides pages, menu items and actions a role cannot use. A denied navigation lands on a "no access" page rather than somewhere unexpected.
- **The API** rejects the request regardless of what the interface showed, so nothing is protected by hiding a button alone.

Two rules apply to managing roles themselves:

- You cannot grant a permission you do not hold yourself.
- Only roles with the relevant management permission can read or edit roles at all.

<Note>
A role marked as the organization's default cannot be edited or deleted, so a workspace can never be left without one to assign new users.
</Note>
